# 08 · Choosing among the three account plans, renewal and payment

LeoTun accounts come in three plans, Personal, Family and Enterprise. They differ only in price and in the number of devices online at the same time; regions, traffic and connection methods are exactly the same on all three. This article explains: what each of the three plans gives and how to choose; which plan a new account is on, how to change plan when buying, and how the remaining time is calculated on a plan change; how the discount works the longer you buy; how to pay with Alipay, PayPal and credit card, and why the page jumps once when paying inside China; who paying by the day with credits suits; how much the free trial and invitation reward are on each plan; and what happens after expiry.

## The three plans at a glance

| | Personal | Family | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | $4 | $8 | $16 |
| Price multiple | 1x | 2x | 4x |
| Devices online at the same time | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| Regions / traffic / connection methods | All / unlimited / all | Same | Same |

- All three plans can use Cisco, OpenVPN, Hiddify, Proxy, Private network and Router, and all can build router firmware. The plan does not decide what you can use, only how many devices can be connected at the same time.
- There is no limit on how many devices you install on; what is limited is the number online at the same moment. A router counts as one device, and the phones and TVs behind it are not counted. When the number online at the same time exceeds the plan's limit, you can temporarily upgrade the account type (remaining time is converted at equal value by price) and switch back when you are done.
- How to choose: one person with a phone plus a computer chooses Personal; a family, or a home with a router installed where you also carry a phone and laptop outside, chooses Family; small teams and those with many devices choose Enterprise. If you are unsure, start with Personal and upgrade if it is not enough; the remaining time is not wasted (see "Changing plan" below).

## New accounts, and choosing a plan when buying

- A newly registered account is on the Personal plan by default, and the free trial and invitation reward are given at the Personal plan's figures.
- The renewal dialog has an "Account plan" selector that defaults to your current plan; whichever price card you click on the home page, the dialog pre-selects that plan, and you can still change it afterwards.
- If you select a different plan, the dialog shows "After payment completes, the account switches automatically to such-and-such plan, and unexpired validity is converted at equal value". Price, discount and number of credits are all calculated live for the plan you selected; what you see is what is actually charged.
- You can also change plan without buying anything: click your username to switch plan.

## Changing plan: remaining time is converted at equal value by price

The rule in one sentence: **remaining time × old plan's multiple ÷ new plan's multiple**. The money stays the same and is exchanged for time on the new plan.

| Now | Remaining | Change to | Remaining after the change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal | 60 days | Family | 30 days |
| Personal | 60 days | Enterprise | 15 days |
| Enterprise | 10 days | Personal | 40 days |

- An account that has already expired gets no conversion on a plan change, and the expiry date does not move; on renewal the plan is switched first, and then the time purchased is added counting from "now".
- When moving down to a plan with fewer devices, if the devices currently online exceed the new plan's limit, you will be prompted to disconnect the extra devices before switching.
- While there is an order that has not been paid, you temporarily cannot change plan; pay it or wait 30 minutes before changing.

## The longer you buy, the cheaper it is

The three plans use the same discount curve: 5% off → 10% off → 20% off. The more expensive plans start earlier: spending the same amount gets you the same discount.

| Plan | 5% off | 10% off | 20% off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal | From 12 months | From 24 months | 48 months |
| Family | From 6 months | From 12 months | From 24 months |
| Enterprise | From 3 months | From 6 months | From 12 months |

- Buying by the month, the most at one time is 12 months; buying by the year, the most at one time is 4 years. Buying by the month gets the same discount once you reach a breakpoint, so you do not have to choose "by the year".
- A few actual figures: Personal 12 months $45.60, 4 years $153.60; Family 6 months $45.60, 2 years $153.60; Enterprise 3 months $45.60, 1 year $153.60.
- If your validity has not yet run out when you renew, the time purchased is added after the original expiry date and does not eat into the remaining days.

## Payment methods

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| Alipay | Payment in RMB, converted from the US dollar price at the live exchange rate |
| PayPal | Paid in US dollars; supports bank cards issued in China |
| Credit card | Visa, Mastercard, AMEX |
| Credits | Deducted from credits you have already topped up; can be used to buy by the day |

- The dialog pre-selects according to the network you are on: Alipay by default in mainland China, PayPal by default elsewhere. It is only a default, and one click changes it.
- Cash (Alipay, PayPal, credit card) buys by the month and by the year; by the day uses credits.

## Paying inside China: click once and finish in the same browser

In mainland China, after you click "Confirm payment", the page first shows "Entering the China payment channel…", logs you in automatically, and then jumps straight to the payment page with the plan, duration and payment method you just selected. For you this means:

- You click only once, the same as paying outside China, with no need to log in again or select again.
- It must be completed in the same browser. This jump only recognises the browser that started the payment; copying the link into another browser, or sending it to someone else to open, will not get in. This is to prevent someone using the link to log in to your account.
- If the channel cannot be entered, the page gives a "Pay on the original site" button: what you selected is kept, you will not be charged twice, and one click lets you pay as usual.

## Credits and paying by the day

- Topping up credits: 25 credits for $8, and you can choose 25–150 credits at a time. Credits never expire.
- Daily consumption: Personal 1 credit per day, Family 2 credits, Enterprise 4 credits. A day on the Personal plan is about $0.32.
- Credits can also renew by the month (counted as 30 days) or by the year (counted as 365 days), but credits get no discount.
- Who it suits: people who use it only ten or so days a month, or who need only a few days before going back to China. If you use it every day, a monthly subscription is better value; on the Personal plan, if you use more than 12 days a month you should subscribe monthly.

## Free trial and invitation reward: equal value on every plan

| | Personal | Family | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free trial | 24 hours | 12 hours | 6 hours |
| For each invited friend's first payment | +30 days | +15 days | +7.5 days |
| Service included with a router purchase (24-month models) | 24 months | 12 months | 6 months |

- Trial: claim it on the home page after registering, no credit card needed; a trial code is sent to your email, and you enter the 6-digit code to activate; once per account. A new account is on the Personal plan, so what you normally get is 24 hours.
- Invitation: after a friend registers through your referral link and completes their first payment, your validity is extended directly, with no limit on the number of people. The days are calculated by the plan you are on at that time.
- These figures are all converted in inverse proportion to the price multiple, so the value you get is the same whichever plan you change to.

## After expiry

- Within the 24 hours before expiry you will receive a "Service about to expire" reminder email.
- After expiry no connection method will connect; devices in the Private network go offline within a few minutes, and the Hiddify QR code can only be regenerated after renewing.
- A day or two after expiry you will receive a "Service has expired" email; for those who qualify it includes a claim button that needs no login and gives 24 / 12 / 6 hours according to your plan; the link is valid for 7 days and can only be clicked once.
- Service is restored directly after renewing; Private network devices each just need login clicked once more, and the exit setting is kept.

## FAQ

**Can the plan be changed at any time? Will I lose out?** Yes; remaining time is converted at equal value by price, and it is just a change of form. Before downgrading, just disconnect the extra devices and settle any unpaid order.

**Should a single user go on the Family plan?** No. The three plans have the same features and the router can be used too; the Personal plan's 2 devices online at the same time are enough for one phone plus one computer. Consider the Family plan only if you have a router at home and also carry two devices with you.

**Why do I see an RMB amount when using Alipay?** Prices are set in US dollars, and Alipay converts to RMB at the live exchange rate at the time of payment, so the RMB amount fluctuates slightly with the exchange rate.

**Can credits and a monthly subscription be used together?** Yes. Credits sit there without expiring; when renewing, choosing "Credits" as the payment method deducts from the balance, and choosing another method pays cash as usual.

**Can I get a refund?** Yes, pro rata by time used; contact a staff member to arrange it.
