# 08 · Choosing an account plan, renewal and payment

LeoTun accounts come in six paid plans: Basic, Standard, Pro, Elite, Enterprise and Ultimate (since 2026-11-11; the old Personal, Family and Enterprise were renamed Basic, Standard and Pro at the same prices), plus a 30-minute free trial. The plans differ only in price, the number of devices online at the same time and how many routers you can bind; regions, traffic and connection methods are exactly the same. This article explains: what each plan gives and how to choose; which plan a new account is on, how to change plan when buying, and how the remaining time is calculated on a plan change; how the discount works the longer you buy; how to pay with Alipay, PayPal and credit card, and why the page jumps once when paying inside China; who paying by the day with credits suits; the free trial and invitation reward; and what happens after expiry.

## The six plans at a glance

| Plan | Monthly price | Devices online at the same time | Routers | Credits/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $5 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Standard | $10 | 4 | 2 | 2 |
| Pro | $20 | 8 | 3 | 4 |
| Elite | $40 | 16 | 4 | 8 |
| Enterprise | $80 | 32 | 5 | 16 |
| Ultimate | $160 | 64 | 6 | 32 |

Each plan costs twice the one below it. Alipay charges in RMB at a fixed rate of US$1 = ¥7 (¥35, ¥70, ¥140, ¥280, ¥560, ¥1120 a month).

- All six plans can use Cisco, OpenVPN, Hiddify, Proxy, Private network and Router, and all can build router firmware; routers have no speed limit on any plan. The plan does not decide what you can use, only how many devices can be connected at the same time and how many routers you can bind.
- There is no limit on how many devices you install on; what is limited is the number online at the same moment. Each router counts as one device, and the phones and TVs behind it are not counted. When the number online at the same time exceeds the plan's limit, you can temporarily upgrade the account type (remaining time is converted at equal value by price) and switch back when you are done.
- How to choose: one person with a phone plus a computer chooses Basic; a family, or a home with a router installed where you also carry a phone and laptop outside, chooses Standard; small teams and those with many devices choose Pro; more people or several offices go higher. If you are unsure, start with Basic and upgrade if it is not enough; the remaining time is not wasted (see "Changing plan" below).
- Old names: Personal = Basic, Family = Standard, and the old $20, 8-device Enterprise = Pro. Today's Enterprise is the new $80, 32-device plan.

## Routers: how many each plan can bind

- Routers one account can bind, by plan: Basic 1, Standard 2, Pro 3, Elite 4, Enterprise 5, Ultimate 6. A new router is bound automatically the first time it connects.
- When all slots are used, the next router cannot connect: unbind one you no longer use under Router on the website, or upgrade your plan. You can unbind an old router even if it is broken or not with you.
- The free trial cannot use a router; once you pay, you can.

## New accounts, and choosing a plan when buying

- A newly registered account is on the Basic plan by default.
- The renewal dialog has an "Account plan" selector that defaults to your current plan; whichever price card you click on the home page, the dialog pre-selects that plan, and you can still change it afterwards.
- If you select a different plan, the dialog shows "After payment completes, the account switches automatically to such-and-such plan, and unexpired validity is converted at equal value". Price, discount and number of credits are all calculated live for the plan you selected; what you see is what is actually charged.
- You can also change plan without buying anything: click your username to switch plan.

## Changing plan: remaining time is converted at equal value by price

The rule in one sentence: **remaining time × old plan's monthly price ÷ new plan's monthly price**. The money stays the same and is exchanged for time on the new plan. Up one plan halves it; down one plan doubles it.

| Now | Remaining | Change to | Remaining after the change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 60 days | Standard | 30 days |
| Basic | 60 days | Pro | 15 days |
| Pro | 10 days | Basic | 40 days |
| Pro | 40 days | Ultimate | 5 days |

- An account that has already expired gets no conversion on a plan change, and the expiry date does not move; on renewal the plan is switched first, and then the time purchased is added counting from "now".
- When moving down to a smaller plan, if the devices online or the bound routers exceed the new plan's limit, you will be prompted to disconnect the extra devices, or unbind the extra routers under Router, before switching.
- While there is an order that has not been paid, you temporarily cannot change plan; pay it or wait 30 minutes before changing.

## The longer you buy, the cheaper it is

All plans use the same discount curve: 5% off → 10% off → 20% off. The more expensive plans start earlier.

| Plan | 5% off | 10% off | 20% off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | From 12 months | From 24 months | 48 months |
| Standard | From 6 months | From 12 months | From 24 months |
| Pro, Elite, Enterprise, Ultimate | From 3 months | From 6 months | From 12 months |

- Buying by the month, the most at one time is 12 months; buying by the year, the most at one time is 4 years. Buying by the month gets the same discount once you reach a breakpoint, so you do not have to choose "by the year". 1 or 2 months get no discount; 20% off is the maximum.
- A few actual figures: Basic 12 months $57, 4 years $192; Standard 6 months $57, 2 years $192; Pro 3 months $57, 1 year $192. Elite, Enterprise and Ultimate each double again, for example Elite 1 year $384, Ultimate 1 year $1536.
- If your validity has not yet run out when you renew, the time purchased is added after the original expiry date and does not eat into the remaining days.

## Payment methods

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| Alipay | Payment in RMB at a fixed rate of US$1 = ¥7: monthly ¥35 Basic, ¥70 Standard, ¥140 Pro, ¥280 Elite, ¥560 Enterprise, ¥1120 Ultimate; 28 credits ¥70 |
| PayPal | Paid in US dollars (monthly Basic $5, Standard $10, Pro $20, Elite $40, Enterprise $80, Ultimate $160); supports bank cards issued in China |
| Credit card | Visa, Mastercard, AMEX |
| Credits | Deducted from credits you have already topped up; can be used to buy by the day |

- The dialog pre-selects according to the network you are on: Alipay by default in mainland China, PayPal by default elsewhere. It is only a default, and one click changes it.
- Cash (Alipay, PayPal, credit card) buys by the month and by the year; by the day uses credits.

## Paying inside China: click once and finish in the same browser

In mainland China, after you click "Confirm payment", the page first shows "Entering the China payment channel…", logs you in automatically, and then jumps straight to the payment page with the plan, duration and payment method you just selected. For you this means:

- You click only once, the same as paying outside China, with no need to log in again or select again.
- It must be completed in the same browser. This jump only recognises the browser that started the payment; copying the link into another browser, or sending it to someone else to open, will not get in. This is to prevent someone using the link to log in to your account.
- If the channel cannot be entered, the page gives a "Pay on the original site" button: what you selected is kept, you will not be charged twice, and one click lets you pay as usual.

## Credits and paying by the day

- Topping up credits: 28 credits for $10, and you can choose 28–168 credits at a time. Credits never expire.
- Daily consumption: Basic 1 credit per day, Standard 2, Pro 4, Elite 8, Enterprise 16, Ultimate 32. A day on the Basic plan is about $0.36.
- Credits can also renew by the month (counted as 30 days) or by the year (counted as 365 days), but credits get no discount.
- Who it suits: people who use it only ten or so days a month, or who need only a few days before going back to China. If you use it every day, a monthly subscription is better value; on the Basic plan, if you use more than 14 days a month you should subscribe monthly.

## Free trial, invitation reward and service included with a router

- Trial: claim it on the home page after registering, no credit card needed; a trial code is sent to your email, and you enter the 6-digit code to activate; once per account; 30 minutes, and the trial cannot use a router.
- Invitation: after a friend registers through your referral link and completes their first payment, you and your friend each get 15 credits, added straight to your credit balance, the same on every plan, with no limit on the number of people. The Basic plan uses 1 credit a day, so 15 credits is about 15 days on Basic, and you can use them to renew by the day.
- The service included with a router purchase (2-year models) is converted in inverse proportion to price, so the value is the same whichever plan you are on: Basic 2 years, Standard 12 months, Pro 6 months, Elite 3 months, Enterprise 1 month; Ultimate gets no extra service.

## After expiry

- Within the 24 hours before expiry you will receive a "Service about to expire" reminder email.
- After expiry no connection method will connect; devices in the Private network go offline within a few minutes, and the Hiddify QR code can only be regenerated after renewing.
- A day or two after expiry you will receive a "Service has expired" reminder email.
- Service is restored directly after renewing; Private network devices each just need login clicked once more, and the exit setting is kept.

## FAQ

**Can the plan be changed at any time? Will I lose out?** Yes; remaining time is converted at equal value by price, and it is just a change of form. Before downgrading, just disconnect the extra devices and routers and settle any unpaid order.

**Should a single user go on the Standard plan?** No. All plans have the same features and the router can be used too; the Basic plan's 2 devices online at the same time are enough for one phone plus one computer. Consider the Standard plan if you have a router at home and also carry two devices with you, or want to bind a second router.

**What is the old Enterprise plan called now?** The old $20, 8-device Enterprise plan is now Pro; accounts were moved over automatically, nothing to do. Today's Enterprise plan is the $80, 32-device plan added on 2026-11-11.

**Why do I see an RMB amount when using Alipay?** Prices are set in US dollars, and Alipay converts to RMB at a fixed rate of US$1 = ¥7 (since 2026-10-21; it no longer follows the live exchange rate): monthly ¥35 Basic ($5), ¥70 Standard ($10), ¥140 Pro ($20), and so on up, with the same volume discounts.

**Can credits and a monthly subscription be used together?** Yes. Credits sit there without expiring; when renewing, choosing "Credits" as the payment method deducts from the balance, and choosing another method pays cash as usual.

**Can I get a refund?** Yes, pro rata by time used; contact a staff member to arrange it.
