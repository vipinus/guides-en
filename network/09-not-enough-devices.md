# 09 · What to do when you run out of device connections

One LeoTun account can be installed on any number of devices; the only limit is the number online at the same time: 2 on the Personal plan, 4 on the Family plan, 8 on the Enterprise plan. When that is not enough, first confirm whether it really is not enough, then see whether it is temporary or long term: for a few extra devices temporarily (relatives and friends visiting, extra devices on a business trip), upgrade one plan temporarily and switch back when you are done; remaining time is converted at equal value by price, and you pay the difference only for those extra days. If it is not enough in the long term, change straight to a higher plan, or use a router to combine the devices at home into one.

For a full explanation of the three plans' prices and the plan-change conversion rules, see [08 · Choosing among the three account plans, renewal and payment](08-account-tiers-and-payment.md).

## Confirm first: is it really not enough

- What is limited is "online at the same time", not how many devices it is installed on. Installed on 10 devices with only 2 on at the same time, the Personal plan is enough.
- The six connection methods share the same quota: a phone with Cisco on and a computer with Hiddify on count as two devices.
- A router counts as only one device; the TVs, set-top boxes and family members' phones behind it are not counted.
- Remember to disconnect devices you are not using. Private network (Tailscale) stays online after login, and a router stays connected too; they keep occupying slots.
- The green number in the status bar at the top of the website is the number of devices currently online; hovering the mouse over it shows your plan's limit.

## Case one: a few extra days temporarily

Relatives or friends staying at your home for a few days, an extra tablet and laptop on a business trip, lending it to a colleague for a while: in cases like these, where you only need more for a few days, upgrading one plan temporarily is the best value. On the way up, remaining time is converted at equal value by price, and it is converted back when you switch back afterwards, so you pay the difference only for the days spent on the higher plan.

1. Log in to the website, click the account plan shown in the status bar, and select the next plan up (Personal → Family → Enterprise). Remaining time is converted by price: up one plan it halves, up two plans it becomes a quarter.
2. Log in and use the new devices directly, with no need to reinstall or change settings.
3. When you are done, first disconnect or log out the extra devices, then click the plan to switch back to the original plan; remaining time is converted back at the same ratio.

## What a temporary upgrade costs: working it out

The three plans are priced 1 : 2 : 4 (Personal $4, Family $8, Enterprise $16 per month), so on a plan change the remaining time is converted at this ratio and nobody loses out. For example: with 30 days left on the Family plan, switching temporarily to the Enterprise plan makes it 15 days; after using 3 days there are 12 days left, and switching back to the Family plan makes it 24 days. Compared with staying on the Family plan throughout (30 − 3 = 27 days) that is 3 days fewer; in other words, those 3 days were charged at the Enterprise plan's price, and what you paid extra is only the difference for those 3 days.

Two restrictions: if a payment was started within the last 30 minutes but not completed, the switch will not go through; complete or abandon that payment and wait half an hour before switching. When switching back to the lower plan, if the devices online still exceed the lower plan's limit, the website will prompt you to disconnect the extra devices first.

## Case two: not enough in the long term

If there are more and more devices at home and family members each need it on their own phones, do not keep switching back and forth; choose one of the two options below.

- Change straight to a higher plan: when renewing, select the target plan in the payment dialog; the switch happens automatically once payment completes, with unexpired time first converted at equal value and then the newly purchased time added. You can also click the plan in the status bar to switch at any time without renewing.
- Use a router: the TV, set-top box, computers and family members' phones at home all connect to the router's Wi‑Fi and count as only one device; add the phone you carry when you go out, and the Personal plan's 2 devices are often enough. All three account plans can use a router.
- How to choose: if the extra devices are mainly at home, use a router; if family members each also need it when they are out (one phone each), upgrade. The two can also be used together.

## FAQ

**Will I lose time by upgrading?** No. Remaining time is converted at equal value by price, and converted back at the same ratio when you switch back; you pay the difference only for the days spent on the higher plan.

**Can I add just one device on its own?** The quota is given by plan: Personal 2, Family 4, Enterprise 8. For a few extra devices temporarily, upgrade one plan temporarily and switch back when you are done.

**My account has expired; can I switch to a lower plan first to get more time?** Conversion applies to time that has not been used up yet; for an account that has already expired, renew first and choose the plan you want when paying.

**What do I do when a new device says the limit has been reached?** Log out or disconnect on an old device you are not using, and the slot is freed; or upgrade one plan temporarily and switch back when you are done.

**Can you force a particular device offline for me?** You can contact support to have it handled; if you can do it yourself, disconnecting or logging out directly on that device is quickest.
