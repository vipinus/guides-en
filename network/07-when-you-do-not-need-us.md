# 07 · Which problems the line solves, and which need another fix

## Why we wrote this

After doing support for a long time we found that **a considerable share of requests for help actually have nothing to do with the line**: looking in a different direction often solves them faster.

Making these situations clear up front saves trouble for both sides.

## In these situations, switching lines will not help

**The site itself is under maintenance or down.**
Nobody can open it, inside China or outside. How to tell: ask someone else to open the same page and see.

**The content needs a membership or payment.**
A greyed-out song is related to copyright and region, but some simply need a membership. If it prompts "become a member", changing IP does not solve it.

**The account has been restricted by the platform.**
Prompts of wrong password, verification required, abnormal account: these are account matters. Changing IP sometimes makes the platform even more nervous.

**You just want to save some data or speed up games.**
What we do is "let you reach it"; we are not a game accelerator. A cross-border line **increases** latency rather than reducing it, and gaming over it is usually laggier.

**Company intranet systems.**
To reach the company's own systems, just use the account and VPN issued by the company.

**Download speed is not what you hoped.**
If you are in China downloading something from China, going through a cross-border line will only be slower. The line is for crossing the border, not for speeding up what is local.

## In these situations, check for yourself before coming to us

**Only one app has a problem, and everything else is normal.**
Eight times out of ten it is that app. First uninstall and reinstall, clear the cache, and check the version.

**It stopped working after you changed to a new phone.**
Most likely the configuration was not imported or an old client version was installed.

**Every device at home is slow.**
Restart the optical modem and the router first. This step solves more problems than you would think.

**Slow only at a certain time of day.**
See [06 · Why it is sometimes fast and sometimes slow](06-why-sometimes-fast-sometimes-slow.md).

## So when do you really need us

In one sentence: when you are **refused because your IP is outside China (or inside China)**.

The typical signs are a prompt of "This content is not available in your region", a page that simply will not open, or friends in China being able to open it when you cannot. This kind of problem is fixed immediately by changing IP, and it is what we really solve.

## Our advice

Use the free trial first to try, one by one, the services you want to use. **If it solves the problem, stay; for other needs, contact support**, and we will help you see whether there is a suitable way.
