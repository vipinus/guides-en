# 04 · For elderly family members and the TV

> Website version (longer, also in Traditional Chinese and English): https://7d24hrs.com/guides/family-tv

## The situation

The elderly people at home only know how to turn on the TV and watch Chinese dramas, and cannot operate a client; TV boxes and smart TVs cannot install the app. Relying on you to talk them through it remotely every time is not realistic.

## The solution: a router

Set up the line for reaching China from abroad on the router and turn on split routing. After that:

- The TV, the TV box and the elderly family member's tablet can watch as soon as they connect to this Wi-Fi, with nothing to install.
- Split routing lets local websites and local devices keep connecting directly; only Chinese video and music go through the VPN, so speed is not affected.

## Two ways to do it

| Approach | Suited to | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Buy a pre-installed router | People who do not want to tinker at all | Plug it in when it arrives and log in to your account once on the admin page |
| Flash the firmware yourself | People who already have a supported router model | Takes a little hands-on ability; the firmware release page is the authority on which models are supported |

## What it is like for the elderly

They do not need to know the word "VPN". iQIYI and Yangshipin (CCTV) on the TV open as usual, just as they would in China. The only thing to tell them: do not unplug the router. After a power cut and restart, it recovers automatically within a minute.

## When the household also needs the local network

In split routing mode, local bank websites, local streaming services and printers keep connecting directly. Only the Chinese domains on the list go through the line for reaching China. This is also why split routing is recommended rather than Global.
