# 05 · Music, podcasts and audiobooks

## What you see

In NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music and Kugou, large blocks of songs are greyed out, with the message "At the request of the rights holder, this cannot be played in your region for now". The same goes for some content on Ximalaya and Qingting FM.

## Cause

As with video, it is regional rights, and only IP is checked. After switching to a China IP, refresh the list and the greyed-out songs come back immediately.

## Notes on each platform

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| NetEase Cloud Music | The strictest in judging IP, but also the most complete once the IP is changed |
| QQ Music | Some songs need a membership, which is not an IP problem |
| Kugou / Kuwo | Changing the IP is enough |
| Ximalaya | Some paid albums are region-restricted |

## Songs you have already downloaded

Some apps also run the region check on the local cache, so songs downloaded before you changed IP may be greyed out all the same. Reopening the app after changing IP is enough; there is no need to download them again.

## A low-effort approach

Music uses little traffic, so the **web proxy** or a browser-based method is enough, and the whole device does not need to go through the line. On a phone you can instead send only the music app through the line (per-app proxy) and keep the other apps on the local network, which saves both data and battery.
