# 09 · Verification codes that never arrive, and Chinese phone numbers

## What you see

SMS verification codes from government services, banks, 12306 and all sorts of apps cannot be sent to your phone, or are sent but never arrive.

## Cause

SMS verification codes are sent only to Chinese phone numbers. When you are overseas: you did not bring the Chinese card with you, or you brought it but did not turn on international roaming, or the service was suspended for unpaid charges. This has nothing to do with IP, and switching to a China IP does not solve SMS.

## Fixes

1. **Keep a Chinese SIM card**, activate international roaming before you leave the country, and choose a number-retention plan. With most carriers, receiving SMS abroad is free or almost free.
2. **A dual-SIM phone**: the Chinese card is used only to receive SMS and the local card for data, with both cards in at the same time.
3. **eSIM**: some carriers support converting a Chinese number to an eSIM, installed on a phone that supports eSIM without taking up a card slot.
4. **Virtual numbers are unreliable**: code-receiving numbers rented online are often blacklisted by platforms. Never use them to register for government services or banks.

## It arrived, but verification fails

- Verification codes are valid only briefly, usually 5 minutes; enter it as soon as it arrives.
- Requesting codes several times in one day gets you rate-limited; wait an hour and try again.
- Face recognition failures are mostly a matter of the app version or the camera permission; install the Chinese edition of the app and allow the camera.
