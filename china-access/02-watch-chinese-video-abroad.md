# 02 · Watching Chinese video from abroad

> Website version (longer): https://7d24hrs.com/guides/overseas-video?utm_source=github&utm_content=china-access-02

## Why you cannot watch

The platforms buy rights for the mainland only, and overseas IPs are blocked across the board. This is not a technical fault, and changing the IP is the only fix.

## Differences between platforms

| Platform | Web version | App | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| iQIYI | Changing the IP is enough | Changing the IP is enough | The overseas edition, iQIYI, is a different app with less content; do not install the wrong one |
| Tencent Video | Changing the IP is enough | Changing the IP is enough | The same goes for the overseas edition, WeTV |
| Youku | Changing the IP is enough | Changing the IP is enough | |
| Mango TV | Changing the IP is enough | Changing the IP is enough | |
| Bilibili | Most content can be watched anyway | Same | Only some anime series and documentaries are region-restricted |
| Yangshipin / CCTV | Changing the IP is enough | Same | Live channels are the most sensitive to IP |

**The overseas-edition app pitfall**: searching an overseas store for the Chinese name of iQIYI usually gets you the iQIYI international edition, whose content library is completely different. To watch the Chinese library you have to install the Chinese edition of the app; iPhone needs a China-region Apple ID, and Android can install the APK directly.

## Connected, but still told there is a copyright restriction

If the exit IP is already mainland China and the site still shows a copyright restriction, nine times out of ten the device's **IPv6 or local DNS is bypassing the line**. Since 2026-09, LeoTun's three whole-device connection methods block IPv6 on the line side; Hiddify configurations imported before September need to be deleted and imported again by scanning the QR code. For the troubleshooting steps, see [IPv6 and DNS are the ones that slip through](../troubleshooting/03-ipv6-and-dns-leak.md).

## Watching on a TV

TVs and set-top boxes cannot install a client, so there are two routes:

1. **The router option**: set up the China-bound line on the router and turn on split routing, and the TV can watch once it is connected to that Wi-Fi. The whole household benefits; see [For elderly family members and the TV](../router/04-family-tv-and-router.md).
2. **Screen casting**: turn on the China-bound line on your phone, then cast to the TV. Simple, but the phone has to stay on the whole time.

## Picture quality

1080p needs a steady 5 Mbps or more. If playback stutters at the evening peak, pick a green-light region or switch connection method; if it still stutters, the usual cause is the local broadband. Choosing an entry point that is close to you and has an optimised line to the mainland works better than changing platform.
