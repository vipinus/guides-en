# Reaching China from Abroad · Everything that needs a China IP

When you are overseas, many Chinese services refuse you because your IP is not in mainland China: video, music, government services, banking, ticket booking, games. One article per situation, explaining clearly **why you are blocked, how to fix it, and what other pitfalls there are**.

**The most-asked article: [Unlocking Chinese video sites from abroad with a router](../router/06-unlock-chinese-video-with-router.md)** — the TV, the set-top box and every device in the household are set up once, and you can watch iQIYI, Tencent Video and Yangshipin as soon as you connect to the Wi-Fi.
