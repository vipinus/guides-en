# 15 · Online courses, exam registration and degree verification

## The situation

You are abroad, but many things to do with "study" have to be done on websites in China:

- Looking up your qualifications on CHSI and issuing an online verification report
- Registering and paying fees on the China Postgraduate Admissions Information Network
- The registration systems for exams such as the teaching qualification, the legal professional exam and the CPA exam
- Some courses on the National Smart Education Platform for Primary and Secondary Schools and on China University MOOC
- A university's own academic administration system and library databases

## Why these sites stall so easily

They are harder to open than commercial websites, for three reasons stacked together:

**First, they serve China only.** Government and education systems have no demand for overseas access, and their server bandwidth is not optimised for cross-border use either.

**Second, registration periods are concentrated.** Hundreds of thousands of people crowd into the same morning. It is slow to begin with, and with the cross-border layer on top the chance of a timeout is extremely high.

**Third, verification codes and uploads are the weak links.** A page that opens is not the same as getting all the way through. The verification code image, the ID card photo upload, the payment redirect: a timeout at any one of these steps sends you back to the start.

## Key advice: for registration, do not settle for "it opens, that will do"

A stutter while watching a video does not matter; a stutter during registration may cost you a year. So:

- **Run through the whole process a day in advance**, including logging in, uploading one image and getting as far as the payment page (without paying), and confirm that no step times out
- **Avoid the first two hours after registration opens**, which is when it is most crowded
- **Use a computer, not a phone**; uploads and verification codes go wrong far less often on a computer
- **Finish in one go and do not disconnect midway**; on many systems the session timeout is only ten-odd minutes

## One special point about CHSI

CHSI's online verification report has a validity period, and **the report page itself also needs a China IP to open**. If you send the report to a university or employer abroad, the link may not open when they click it. The safe approach is to open it yourself on a China IP, **export it as a PDF**, and send that.

## Paying fees

Registration fees usually redirect to UnionPay or Alipay. This step is as sensitive to the environment as online banking is, so we suggest:

- Keep the same IP throughout, and do not switch region midway
- Keep the mobile banking app and the registration page on the computer in the same network environment as far as possible

For more points to watch with online banking, see [04 · Online banking and payments](04-banking-and-payments.md).

## If your university has its own VPN

Quite a few universities give students a campus VPN for reaching library databases. That is a different thing from what is discussed here, and the two do not conflict: the campus VPN solves "whether you have permission to use this database", but you first have to be able to reach the university's servers. The two can be used on top of each other.
