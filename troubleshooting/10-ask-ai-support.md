# 10 · How to communicate effectively with AI support

> Website version (longer): https://www.leotun.com/guides/ai-support?utm_source=github&utm_content=troubleshooting-10

The AI support agent "SpongeBob" (海绵宝宝) answers 24 hours a day in the three groups on Telegram, QQ and Discord (for the links see [05](05-how-to-reach-us.md)). Whether it answers accurately depends mainly on **whether your first message describes the situation completely**. If it does, you get the steps in one go; if you only say "can't connect", all it can do is ask you questions back. **If there is an error, just send a screenshot. You can ask in any language.**

## Give all four of these in your first message

1. **Device and system**: "Windows 11 laptop", "iPhone, iOS 18", "Android, Xiaomi 14", "GL.iNet router".
2. **Connection method and region**: Private network, Cisco, OpenVPN, Proxy, Hiddify or Router; and which region you chose.
3. **Symptom and error**: cannot connect at all, connects but slow, or drops after a while; if there is an error, **send a screenshot of the error directly** — it can read it. If a particular website or app does not work, give its name.
4. **What you have already tried**: switched regions, switched methods, restarted, switched from Wi‑Fi to mobile data — mention each of them.

## Good and bad ways to ask

| Not good | Good |
|---|---|
| Can't connect | Windows 11, Cisco connecting to Japan, it keeps spinning and finally says the connection timed out, same with Korea, home broadband is China Unicom |
| Very slow | iPhone using Hiddify, Singapore selected, web pages open but video keeps buffering, especially noticeable after 9 pm |
| How do I do it | I just bought the Family plan and want the Xiaomi TV at home to be able to use it too. Is there a way? |
| Asking about renewal, switching regions and the router in one message | Ask one thing at a time, and ask the next once it is solved |

## How to get it to reply in each of the three groups

| Group | How to ask |
|---|---|
| QQ group | **@海绵宝宝** when you ask. When someone is already handling your question it deliberately stays out of the conversation; if you want it to carry on answering, @ it once more |
| Telegram group, Discord | Just ask, no @ needed. It will respond even to "are you there" |

**You can ask in any language**: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Russian and so on. It answers in the language you asked in. There is no need to repeat the same question in all three groups.

## Every reply comes with a voice message

Every reply from SpongeBob is sent together with a voice message, **prepared specifically for people with poor eyesight and for older people**: if reading is a strain, just listen; the speaking pace has been deliberately slowed down. Older family members who find typing difficult can send it a screenshot and then listen to the voice reply. Users who read the text can simply ignore the voice message.

## What it can do, and what needs a human

| Ask the AI | Ask a human (@ the group owner in the QQ group, or use the support email / human support QQ on the Contact page) |
|---|---|
| Registration and trial, prices and points, renewal and switching plans | Processing refunds, payments that have not arrived, duplicate charges |
| How to install each connection method, which region to choose | Banned accounts, authentication failures, router binding conflicts |
| Troubleshooting can't connect, slow, dropped connections, or a website or app that does not work | Router shipping, delivery, damage and warranty |
| Network settings on computers, phones and TV boxes; apps that will not install | Questions where the AI says "Not sure, please @ the group owner" |

VPN connection addresses are taken from the website: after logging in, click the region's flag in the relevant section to copy it.

## Security reminders

- Do not post passwords, verification codes or payment screenshots in the groups, and do not post configuration files, QR codes or subscription links either — they are equivalent to your account.
- Before sending a screenshot to support, blur out the connection addresses and tokens in it.
- Support will never message you privately to ask for a password, a verification code or a payment, and will never ask you on their own initiative to install remote control software.
